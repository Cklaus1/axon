# Bounded self-improvement pilot — 0.22

**Experiment template, not executed results.** The machine-readable [plan](../fixtures/closed_loop_v022/pilot-template.json) is intentionally not runtime-ready: operator approval and statistical/task parameters are unset, deployment is false, and evidence is empty.

## Question and initial surface

Does a new tool/skill shortlist policy reduce complete-task cost without violating the prespecified quality/security constraints on the declared coding-task distribution? The model/role, tool effects, authority, compute profile, verification method, task budget and context policy remain fixed.

The candidate may reorder/narrow known eligible IDs only. It cannot alter permission enforcement, verifier/executable selection, hidden tests, training/confirmation split, activation logic or task interpretation. The incumbent is a pinned existing/deterministic shortlist, not an artificially crippled benchmark target.

## Before any protected outcomes

The owner must fill and approve: task/repository manifests; discovery/tuning/confirmation/reporting roles; mutation proposal budget and candidate IDs; base/environment/model/profile/price pins; repetitions and blocking/randomization; warm/cold and mutable-cache rules; statistical method/version; independent sampling unit; target sample/horizon; quality noninferiority margin; economic benefit threshold; uncertainty level; multiplicity/sequential policy; handling of failure/cancellation/missing cost; data-use policy; active scope and rollback policy.

There is no universal percentage or sample size supplied by the sources. The pack does not invent one. Sample size must reflect workload variance and the desired effect/risk, not a goal of ensuring the new technique wins. Repeated trials must be clustered or handled by a valid preregistered design. A final reporting set cannot double as continual training feedback.

## Run procedure

Freeze the plan and eligible candidate set. Generate/record one bounded mutation using discovery data. Critique it for benchmark-specific behavior and forbidden edits before spending evaluation budget. Materialize identical isolated trial bases and dedicated mutable caches. Observe preflight independently. Execute both arms through real MiCode and qualified Fabric with assigned order and full receipts. Run the independent evaluator on exact outputs. Keep every assigned task and all attempts, including failed or lost work.

Apply the frozen rule; report accepted, rejected or inconclusive. Independent admission is necessary even when a candidate's score is highest. Activate only the exact admitted policy within its approved scope. Observe a later task's actual effective version. Inject a clearly labeled regression/fault to prove rollback/pause; do not mix these synthetic fault trials into the scientific improvement estimate.

## Completion semantics

**Mechanism qualification**: the actual system handles dispatch, rejection, authentication, accounting, adoption and rollback correctly. It may use a separately owner-approved mechanism-test policy to exercise an activation path. That policy must be labeled and excluded from learned-benefit claims.

**Improvement evidence**: a protected study supports the preregistered quality/cost claim with complete outcomes and uncertainty. A mechanism fixture, a paper benchmark, a lower training loss or a successful single anecdote does not establish that claim. Rejected/inconclusive is an honest and expected result.

## Stop and rollback

Stop on authority/profile drift, receipt mismatch, hidden-test exposure, untracked side effects, unknown over-budget liability, resource leakage, cross-arm contamination, current-epoch mismatch or a critical quality regression. Revalidate the predecessor at rollback; pause when no safe eligible predecessor exists. Keep events/receipts and pending financial obligations for reconciliation rather than deleting the evidence.
