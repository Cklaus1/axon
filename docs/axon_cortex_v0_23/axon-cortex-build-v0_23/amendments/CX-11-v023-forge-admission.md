# CX-11 v0.23 Forge integration amendment — Forge admission boundary

Forge compiler/program candidates are subject to the same independent admission rules as other learned capabilities. Semantic conformance and hard constraints are prerequisites, not optimization objectives.

## v0.23 acceptance gates

**G11-r23-forge-admission:** A Forge candidate with failed semantic/security/acceptance invariants is refused by admission regardless of runtime, cost or judge preference improvements.
