## What a FIX does not close

* The **omission** half of every exit-shaped finding (15, 31, 32, 34, 45): a candidate that makes the operator's store be SKIPPED (a swallowed panic, an aborting handler arm) leaves nothing to mark, exactly as the taken `return` does. What is tainted is every store the operator makes after the point, and the taken-or-not branch of an exit; the table of precomputed verdicts and the omission cases stay in the standing residuals.
* Executed instances only. A fix closes the executed shape and the mechanism named in its row; a sibling construct no pass reached is not claimed. The loop did not go dry (pass 6 found 6 more findings; see the evidence statement in the claim).
* 48's `ai_cost_spent` and virtual-clock instances were not executed against the fixed tree; the file instance was.
